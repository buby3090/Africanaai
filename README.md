# AfricanaAI

## Vision

AfricanaAI is an initiative focused on advancing Artificial Intelligence for African languages and under-represented linguistic communities. The project aims to bridge the gap between modern AI technologies and African languages by developing datasets, benchmarks, language models, educational tools, and multilingual AI applications.

## Mission

To create inclusive AI technologies that empower African communities through language accessibility, digital inclusion, education, and innovation.

## Focus Areas

* Natural Language Processing (NLP)
* Large Language Models (LLMs)
* Machine Translation
* Educational AI
* Speech Technologies
* African Language Datasets
* Multilingual AI Systems
* Benchmark Development

## Current Projects

### AfricanaAI Study Assistant

An AI-powered learning assistant that helps students understand concepts in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Programming.

### Hausa Language Dataset Initiative

Development and curation of culturally-aware Hausa datasets for NLP and LLM research.

### Under-Represented Language Benchmark

A benchmark framework for evaluating language technologies on low-resource African languages.

## Research Interests

* Low-resource NLP
* Multilingual Language Models
* Language Preservation
* AI for Education
* Responsible AI
* African Language Technologies

## Long-Term Goal

To establish AfricanaAI as a leading platform for African language technologies and contribute to the development of inclusive AI systems that serve diverse linguistic communities worldwide.
