# AfricanaAI Research Roadmap

## Vision

To build world-class AI technologies for African languages through research, datasets, benchmarks, and multilingual language models.

---

## Phase 1 (Completed)

* Literature review
* Research topic selection
* Initial Hausa dataset collection
* AI prototype development

---

## Phase 2 (In Progress)

* Dataset expansion
* Dataset annotation
* Benchmark design
* Evaluation framework

---

## Phase 3

* Fine-tune multilingual LLMs
* Evaluate existing foundation models
* Publish benchmark

---

## Phase 4

* Develop translation systems
* Educational AI assistant
* Speech technologies

---

## Phase 5

* Multimodal African language models
* Large-scale multilingual datasets
* Public benchmark leaderboard

---

## Long-Term Goals

* Publish research in leading AI conferences and journals.
* Build an open research platform for African language technologies.
* Contribute to responsible and inclusive AI.
* Establish AfricanaAI as a leading research initiative in Africa.
