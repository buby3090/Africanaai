# Future Goals

AfricanaAI is being developed as a long-term research and innovation initiative. The following milestones outline the planned growth of the project.

## Short-Term Goals (1–2 Years)

* Expand Hausa language datasets.
* Develop benchmark datasets for under-represented languages.
* Improve multilingual translation systems.
* Build AI-powered educational applications.
* Publish research papers in NLP and Artificial Intelligence.
* Release open-source tools for African language processing.

## Medium-Term Goals (3–5 Years)

* Support additional African languages such as Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Fulfulde, and Kanuri.
* Develop multilingual Large Language Models.
* Build speech recognition and text-to-speech systems.
* Create evaluation leaderboards for African language benchmarks.
* Establish partnerships with universities and research institutions.

## Long-Term Goals (5–10 Years)

* Build one of Africa's leading AI research platforms for language technologies.
* Develop multimodal AI systems capable of processing text, speech, and images in African languages.
* Create open research infrastructure for African AI.
* Contribute to global AI standards for low-resource languages.
* Support language preservation and digital inclusion across Africa.

## Ultimate Goal

AfricanaAI aims to empower millions of African language speakers by making Artificial Intelligence accessible, inclusive, and culturally relevant while contributing high-quality research to the global AI community.
