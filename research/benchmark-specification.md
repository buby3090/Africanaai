# Benchmark Specification

## Project Title

Benchmark for Evaluating Large Language Models on Under-Represented African Languages

---

## Overview

This benchmark is designed to evaluate the performance of Natural Language Processing (NLP) systems and Large Language Models (LLMs) on under-represented African languages. The benchmark focuses on measuring language understanding, text generation, translation, reasoning, and culturally appropriate responses.

---

## Objectives

* Evaluate multilingual language models.
* Measure performance on low-resource languages.
* Promote reproducible AI research.
* Support inclusive language technologies.

---

## Task Categories

* Machine Translation
* Question Answering
* Text Summarization
* Text Classification
* Named Entity Recognition
* Dialogue Generation
* Cultural Understanding
* Instruction Following

---

## Dataset

Current language:

* Hausa

Future expansion:

* Yoruba
* Igbo
* Swahili
* Fulfulde
* Kanuri

---

## Evaluation Metrics

* Accuracy
* Precision
* Recall
* F1 Score
* BLEU
* ROUGE
* BERTScore
* Human Evaluation

---

## Future Work

The benchmark will expand to additional African languages and multimodal evaluation tasks involving text, speech, and images.
